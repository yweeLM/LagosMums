The format of the show

Here's how a typical episode flows from start to finish.

  1. Intro of the topic

    We open by introducing the topic for the episode.

  2. What and why

    We talk about what the topic is and why it's being discussed.

  3. You lay the framework

    Set out where you'll take listeners. For example: "I will be sharing 5 main takeaways or tips on xyz." Please make it relatable to our clime, a Nigerian audience.

  4. Listener questions

    If there's time, you'll take questions and answer on air. Questions come in via calls, WhatsApp or Twitter, and the co-host from the radio station will read them to you.

  5. Be flexible

    The co-host can go in any direction as the show flows. However…

  6. …stay focused

    Tie everything back to your intro. If you said you'd share 5 takeaways or tips, make sure that before the end of the show you reiterate or complete all 5 main points.

  7. Share your details

    You'll be asked to share your details. You can also mention LagosMums as a resource for parents.

  8. No direct advertising

    As a guest, you can't directly advertise your services. We invited you as an expert, and this is not a paid advert segment. If you'd like to advertise your services, we're happy to discuss it with you at LagosMums.

  9. Recording and clips

    The show is recorded. We sometimes share clips on our social media platforms or turn the conversation into content for our blog.
